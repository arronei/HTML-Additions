Linked form controls

Live versions of the examples in the Linked Form Controls spec, running on link-attribute.js. Under each form is what the form would submit, updated as you type.

Phone number

Type 5551234567, then try Backspace in an empty box, the arrow keys, or pasting 8005550199. (Pasting a formatted number such as (555) 123-4567 spreads the punctuation too; that's an open issue in the spec.)
